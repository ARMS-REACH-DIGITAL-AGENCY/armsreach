Questions from the dugout
Does anyone's paycheck go down?

No. Employee pay and benefits stay exactly the same. PCMP restructures how payroll is handled, not what your people take home.

What does it cost my business?

AW Sales structures PCMP at zero net cost to the employer and zero net cost to the employee. Michael will walk you through exactly how the numbers work for your company.

How does it actually work?

On each paycheck it runs in four steps: a pre-tax contribution, the tax savings it creates, a SIMRP reimbursement, and the net effect. The paycheck example in the box score shows the before and after, and Michael will walk you through it for your payroll.

Is it compliant?
IRS § 213(d)IRS § 125IRS § 105(h)SIMRPCPA reviewedTax attorney approvedAudit defensible

It's independently administered and built to withstand scrutiny. Ask Michael for the written plan documents to review with your own CPA.

What do you need from me to start?

A payroll census and employee IDs. There are small business and corporate versions.

How does the team donation work?

Once your savings are confirmed, you can choose to put a share toward one or more high school baseball programs, given in your company's name. It's money you didn't know you had, so there's no check to write from your budget. It's always optional, and you pick the school and the amount.

Who's eligible?

Two qualifications: you have W-2 employees and a healthcare plan. Your call with Michael confirms the details.
