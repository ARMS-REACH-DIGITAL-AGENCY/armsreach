The separate `mike_crozite_template` repository continues to power the network of school microsites such as `hamilton.az.yatstats.com`. It should not be replaced by the YAT?STATS corporate homepage in this repository.

## Brand structure

- **ARMS REACH Digital Agency**: custom strategy, branding, websites, implementation, partnerships, and fractional growth leadership.
- **ARMS REACH Complete**: productized, ready-to-run Automated Relationship Management System with industry editions and automated onboarding.
- **YAT?STATS**: baseball community and Fan Advancement ecosystem connecting schools, players, alumni, fans, and local co-branding partners.
- **REACH Golf Media / GCTV**: owned golf audiences and measurable media activation.
- **Pete Is My Agent**: selected sales, partnership, and business-development representation.
