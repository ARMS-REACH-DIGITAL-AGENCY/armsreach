Billing and receipts

Common questions

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. ARMS REACH never sees or stores your full card number.

I got a receipt from "YAT?STATS powered by ARMS REACH Digital Agency." What is it?

YAT?STATS is part of the ARMS REACH family, so YAT?STATS purchases are billed through ARMS REACH Digital Agency. If you do not recognize a charge, contact us with the receipt number and we will look it up with you.

Where do I find my receipt number?

It is near the top of the receipt email, right under "Receipt from…", and looks like #1234-5678. The receipt also shows the amount, the date paid, and the last four digits of the card used.

I need a copy of my receipt or an invoice for my records.

Open the original receipt email and choose "View it in your browser" to print or save it. If you cannot find the email, send us your name, the payment date, and the amount and we will resend it.

I think I was charged the wrong amount, or charged twice.

Email or call us with the receipt number(s), the date, and the last four digits of the card. We will review the transaction and follow up with you directly.

How do I update my card or cancel a subscription?

Send a support request with the email address on your account and what you would like to change. We will confirm the change with you before anything is updated.
