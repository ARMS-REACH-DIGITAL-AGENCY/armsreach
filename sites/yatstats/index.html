1,025 school hubs

Each school becomes a local growth engine.

YAT?STATS equips programs with ARMS—the Alumni Relationship Management Solution—to grow and nurture their communities. Schools can introduce local businesses to a stronger co-branding opportunity than a traditional booster donation. Those partners subsidize the platform, receive measurable hometown exposure, and may also become ARMS Complete clients for their own businesses.

Illustrative revenue model: 1,025 hubs × $1,000 per month = $1,025,000 in potential monthly recurring revenue. This is a target model, not a claim that the programs or revenue are currently contracted.
