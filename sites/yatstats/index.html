A different starting point

Following a player is useful. Bringing him home is the bigger idea.

There are plenty of ways to look up baseball stats. YAT?STATS is organized around where a player's story began — the high school community that still cares what happens next.

The typical player tracker

Starts with the player or current team and answers what is happening now.

  • Current rosters
  • Scores and statistics
  • Transfers and schedules

The YAT?STATS hometown model

Starts with the program and connects what happened before, what is happening now, and who still cares.

  • Every alumni journey organized back to his high school
  • Active + inactive alumni in one program history
  • Fan memories, stories and hometown nostalgia
  • Community, fundraising and local sponsorship potential

FAQ

What exactly is YAT?STATS?

Is YAT?STATS another college baseball stats app?

No. Stats are part of the utility, but the organizing principle is the high school program. YAT?STATS brings college and professional alumni back into a hometown context so fans, families, coaches, former teammates and alumni can follow the journey together.

Who is YAT?STATS for?

Fans and families who want to follow hometown players; coaches and booster leaders who want stronger alumni relationships; active and former players who want to stay connected; and local businesses that want to support a meaningful community asset.

What happens after a player stops playing?

He remains part of the program's history. The long-term vision includes all-time alumni lists, memories, stories, interviews, business connections, mentorship and community participation after the real-time stat tracking ends.

Does every school already have a live community?

No. The national database can identify programs, while individual school communities are being developed and activated in stages. Hamilton, Arizona serves as the flagship proof-of-concept environment.

Can fans contribute stories and memories?

That is part of the Golden Timeline vision: allowing people who lived a player's journey to contribute photos, stories and memories and attribute them to the relevant player, season and program.

Where do you fit in?

Every baseball community already exists. YAT?STATS gives it a home.

This preview is the beginning of a cleaner public story. The next step is connecting each audience to the right HighLevel lead path without making the homepage feel like four different websites fighting for attention.
