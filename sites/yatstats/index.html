FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is YAT?STATS just another college baseball stats app?

No. Stats are part of the utility, but the organizing principle is the hometown program. YAT?STATS brings college and professional alumni back into the context of the school, coaches, families, teammates and fans who already care about them.

Who is YAT?STATS built for?

Fans and families, coaches and booster leaders, current and former players, and local businesses that want to support a meaningful baseball community.

What happens after a player stops playing?

He still belongs to the program's history. The broader vision includes all-time alumni lists, memories, stories, interviews, mentorship, business connections and community participation after real-time tracking ends.

Does every school already have a live community?

No. The national database can identify programs while school communities are activated in stages. Hamilton, Arizona is the flagship proof-of-concept environment.

Will YAT?STATS still connect to HighLevel / ARMS?

Yes. The public site can live on a modern Vercel front end while HighLevel handles forms, CRM, attribution, email, SMS, pipelines and audience-specific nurture campaigns behind the scenes.

Where do you fit?

Every baseball community already exists. YAT?STATS gives it a home.

This test build is designed so the final calls-to-action can route fans, coaches, players and local partners into the right ARMS / HighLevel nurture path without forcing the whole public site to live inside the page builder.
