Frequently asked questions

What business owners usually want to know.

Is ARMS Complete just another CRM?

No. A CRM is part of it, but ARMS Complete connects contact management with conversations, booking, automated follow-up, sales pipelines, reviews, websites, forms, and reactivation.

How is this different from ARMS REACH Digital Agency?

ARMS Complete is a standardized subscription with automated onboarding, industry editions, and minimal customization. ARMS REACH Digital Agency is the white-glove option for businesses that need strategy, branding, custom websites, specialized automation, campaigns, or fractional growth leadership.

Do I need technical experience?

No. The purpose of the guided onboarding and training is to help a business owner or team member use the core system without becoming a software expert.

Can ARMS work with my existing website?

Yes. Forms, calendars, chat, tracking, and lead capture can be connected to an existing website. ARMS Complete also includes tools for focused landing pages and funnels.

What happens if I need custom work later?

You can keep ARMS Complete as the operating system and engage ARMS REACH Digital Agency separately for custom strategy, creative work, advanced automation, integrations, or ongoing growth leadership.
